guides

How facts become membershipFacts are written relationships. A namespace's rewrites turn them into the subjectsets that checks read.
A worked example: files, folders and groupsFollow one file's viewers up through its folders and into a group, then see who a ban takes out.
Writing a namespaceDeclare relations and their rewrites, then write a version. Older versions stay readable.
Answering whyRead a check's grounds one by one to see why a subject is or isn't in a subjectset.
Staging and writingChanges stay staged as you move between pages. They land at one revision, or none do.
